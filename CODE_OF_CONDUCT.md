# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Disagreement about security or
design is welcome; harassment, intimidation, discrimination, doxxing, threats,
and deliberate disclosure of another person's sensitive data are not.

Examples of welcome behavior include:

- challenging a claim with reproducible evidence;
- acknowledging uncertainty and correcting mistakes;
- keeping criticism about the work rather than the person; and
- protecting the privacy of users, researchers, and maintainers.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, review
channels, and public events where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, restrict participation, or ban
participants whose conduct is unsafe or persistently disruptive. Serious abuse
may also be reported through GitHub's platform reporting tools.

Do not use a public conduct report to disclose a vulnerability or personal
data. Use the private process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for security issues.
